Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients - Steps

Ingredients

1/2 cup of Softend Butter, 3/4 cup of Brown Sugar, 1 Large Egg, 1 3/4 cup of FLour, 1 cup CHoclate Chips, 2tsp of Vanila Extract

Steps

Preheat Oven to 350 Degrees Farenhight - Mix The Butter, Sugar, Eggs, and Vanila Extract until fully smooth - Add the flour and stir until you cant see it anymore - Fold in the CHocolate Chips - Scoop the dough into balls and place on a baking sheet - Place in the oven and bake for 10-12 Minutes

Fun Fact

Chocolate chip cookies were invented by accident — Ruth Wakefield originally thought the chocolate pieces would melt into the dough, but they kept their shape, creating the signature gooey chunks we love today
