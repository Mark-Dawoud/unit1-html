Chocolate CHip Cookie Recipir

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup of Softend Butter
  • 3/4 cup of Brown Sugar
  • 1 Large Egg
  • 1 3/4 cup of FLour
  • 1 cup CHoclate Chips
  • 2tsp of Vanila Extract

Steps

  1. Preheat Oven to 350 Degrees Farenhight
  2. Mix The Butter, Sugar, Eggs, and Vanila Extract until fully smooth
  3. Add the flour and stir until you cant see it anymore
  4. Fold in the CHocolate CHips
  5. Scoop the dough into balls and place on a baking sheet
  6. Place in the oven and bake for 10-12 Minutes

Notes

  • Equipment Needed
    • large Bowl for mixing
    • Large oven safe baking sheet
  • Serving suggestions
    • If you roll the doing into about 1.5 Tbsp balls before serving, the recepie makes about 12-14 cookies
    • The serving size is about 1 - 2 cookies per person
